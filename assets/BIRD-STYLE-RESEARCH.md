# Pirots 3 art direction — 10 October 2026

The user rejected the first bird family as too humanoid, then confirmed Pirots 3 as the correct reference for the entire pack.

## Primary sources
- [Pirots 3, ELK Studios](https://www.elk-studios.com/games/pirots3/): official Western game page.
- [Official collector group artwork](https://www.elk-studios.com/app/uploads/2024/05/group-pirots-1600x800.png): all four collectors and bandit inspected in browser; research screenshot saved in references.
- [Official Pirots 3 logo](https://www.elk-studios.com/app/uploads/2024/05/pirots3_logoexportreduced-e1716899482947.png): inspected in browser.
- User's original Pirots 3 board screenshot: primary authority for sprites, props, materials and background rendering.

Pirots 4 and 5 were inspected during identification but are not style authorities for this revision. The observations below are visual interpretation, not an ELK style guide.

## Revised contract
- Blue: small round fluffy collector, uneven startled eyes, smaller yellow beak.
- Green: squat collector, wide unequal eyes, heavy brows, medium curved beak.
- Purple: swept crest, skeptical heavy eyelids, strong curved yellow beak, close head accessory.
- Red: huge hooked yellow beak, half-lidded gaze, pale cheek tufts, small dark cowboy hat.
- Rival: hunched charcoal vulture, dark beak, pale neck ruff, tiny top hat.
- Compact heads and bodies, tiny feet, no tall human anatomy.
- Max likeness via eye spacing, brow shape, smiling eye creases, narrow cheek form and a small swept brown feather quiff.
- Rich painted 2.5D shading, chunky feather groups, strong dark contours and broad shiny highlights.
- Gems: simple strong coloured silhouettes and broad facets, with a subtle shallow Max cameo.
- Props: humble steel/ceramic/brass forms close to the screenshot; remove excess jeweled decoration.
- Backgrounds: Pirots 3's theatrical painted treatment, adapted to the user's Venserpolder/Zuidoost theme.
- Original MAX BIRDS title, no franchise lettering or branding in deliverable artwork.
